I'm buying a resale 2BHK in Whitefield. Which documents should I ask the seller for?
For a resale flat in Bangalore, ask for these before you pay an advance:
  1. Sale deed of the seller, plus the earlier deeds (mother deed chain)
  2. Encumbrance Certificate for 30 years
  3. Khata certificate and extract, and the latest tax receipts
  4. Occupancy certificate and approved building plan
  5. Society NOC and the share certificate
