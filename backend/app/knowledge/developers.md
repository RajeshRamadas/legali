# Developer services

## Developer lifecycle
For real-estate developers, Homexo Legal covers the whole lifecycle:
- Land and acquisition: screen parcels, reconstruct title and identify material gaps before committing.
- Registration execution: coordinate documents, deed readiness, SRO appointments and registration-day tasks.
- Mutation and records: track mutation, Khata, tax and utility name-change follow-up after registration.
- Buyer handover: give buyers a clearer checklist, visible progress and organised ownership documents.
Scope, capacity and fees are confirmed for each engagement.

## Land Screening
For business development and land acquisition teams. Initial title and ownership checks, encumbrance and land-classification review, survey identity and document-gap flags, and a preliminary risk summary. Service: Developer Land Screening (developer-land-screening).

## Acquisition Due Diligence
For legal and acquisition leaders. Historical title-chain reconstruction, revenue, survey and EC reconciliation, litigation and restriction checks, and a development-rights review with a lawyer report. Service: Acquisition Due Diligence (acquisition-due-diligence).

## Registration Operations
For projects with multiple unit sales. A unit-level readiness checklist, buyer and seller document coordination, deed and SRO appointment coordination, and registration progress and deed collection tracking. Service: Bulk Registration Operations (bulk-registration-operations).

## Mutation and Records Hygiene
For portfolio and post-sale teams. Mutation application follow-up, Khata and property-tax updates, utility transfer coordination where applicable, and a pending-task dashboard with a document archive. Service: Mutation & Records Management (mutation-records-management).
