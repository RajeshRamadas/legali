# How a case works

## Requesting a free callback
Customers start by requesting a free callback on the website, in the chat assistant or in the mobile app. They share their name, phone number, optional email, property type, property location and the service they need. Every request gets a ticket number in the form HX-YYMMDD-XXXXXX, for example HX-261018-ABC123. A specialist then calls to confirm scope, documents and fees. Nothing is charged before scope and fees are confirmed with the customer.

## Stages of a case
1. Order placed: the customer chooses a package, shares property details and uploads the documents they have. The secure document checklist is shared.
2. Title review: the team reviews the title chain, EC, RERA, approvals and available litigation and acquisition records.
3. Record verification: where included and feasible, the team coordinates physical SRO checks and shares progress.
4. Verdict prepared: evidence, conflicts and open questions are organised for the advocate's review.
5. Next step: the customer receives the reviewed report and guidance on proceeding, resolving issues or investigating further.

## Ticket statuses customers see
- Request received: the request is in and a specialist will call to confirm scope.
- Specialist reviewing scope: the team is checking what the case needs.
- Waiting for your reply: the team needs something from the customer, such as a document or an answer. The customer can reply from Track your request or from the chat.
- Scope confirmed: scope and fees are agreed.
- Case team assigned: a case team member is handling the request.
- Work in progress: checks are underway.
- In advocate review: findings are with the advocate for the final review.
- Completed: the work is done.
- On hold, Reopened, Cancelled or Not taken up may also appear.

## Tracking a request
Customers can track a request in the Track your request section of the website, or ask the chat assistant. They need the ticket number and the phone number or email they used when they raised it. The tracker shows the current stage, the history of updates and messages from the case team, and lets the customer reply to the team while the case is open.

## Status updates and notifications
Status updates reflect the case team's recorded progress and confirmed source checks. They are not a live connection to any government portal. Customers receive SMS, email and WhatsApp-friendly updates when the team records a stage change or sends a message.

## Professional review and limits
Automated tools can organise evidence, but legal conclusions and final opinions are given by qualified advocates. No service can guarantee that every possible risk or dispute will be found. The verdict explains what was verified, the material risks, missing information and the conditions to address before proceeding.
