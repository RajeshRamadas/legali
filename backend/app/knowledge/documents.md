# Document desk

## Government document retrieval and courier
Customers tell us the document they need and the property location. The team confirms the issuing authority, eligibility, authorisation, timeline and charges, then coordinates the request and couriers physical documents to the customer. Service: Government Document Retrieval & Courier (document-retrieval-courier).

## Land and revenue records
RTC / Pahani, Mutation Register extracts, mutation orders and eligible survey records. The team also follows up on mutation after registration. Service: RTC / Mutation / Survey Records (rtc-mutation-survey-records).

## Registration documents and certified copies
Encumbrance Certificate (EC), registered deed copies and other eligible certified-copy requests. Service: Certified Copy / Encumbrance Certificate (certified-copy-ec).

## Khata and property records
Khata and e-Khata guidance, property-tax records and application follow-up. Service: Khata / Property Tax Documents (khata-property-tax-documents).

## Legal documents and deeds
Sale agreement, sale deed, POA, Gift Deed and other property-document drafting or vetting support. Service: Deed Drafting / Registration Support (deed-drafting-registration).
