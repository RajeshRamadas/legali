# Services

## Title verification and Encumbrance Certificate
The team reviews the title deed chain, Mother Deed, Encumbrance Certificate and relevant transfer records, and flags gaps and potential conflicts for the advocate. The outcome is a plain-English Go / Investigate / Do Not Proceed verdict. Service: Title Verification (title-verification).

## Approval and compliance audit
The team checks applicable RERA registration, BBMP / BDA / BMRDA approvals, zoning, occupancy and commencement certificates (OC / CC) and available land acquisition notices. Part of Shield Pro and Full Legal Due Diligence (legal-due-diligence).

## SRO field verification
Where the package includes it (Shield Pro and Shield Complete), the team coordinates a physical check of the relevant Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) records and reports the confirmed findings.

## Drafting and vetting of agreements and deeds
The team coordinates sale agreement, sale deed and buyer-protective clauses with the responsible advocate, based on the transaction. It also assists with Power of Attorney (POA), Gift Deed, Will registration and other property deeds, subject to professional review. Services: Sale Agreement / Sale Deed (sale-agreement-deed) and Deed Drafting / Registration Support (deed-drafting-registration).

## Property registration
The team prepares a readiness checklist, coordinates the transaction paperwork, the deed and the SRO appointment, and supports the customer on registration day. Government fees and statutory outcomes remain separate. Service: Property Registration (property-registration).

## Post-purchase care: Khata, property tax and BESCOM
After registration the team supports Khata transfer, property-tax name change and BESCOM (electricity) name transfer where applicable. Shield Complete includes these. Service: Khata / Tax / BESCOM Transfer (khata-tax-bescom-transfer).

## Khata and tax services
Assistance with A Khata, B Khata, BDA Khata, e-Khata / e-Swathu, BBMP e-Aasthi and property-tax processes, including Khata and property-tax documents and application follow-up. Service: Khata / Property Tax Documents (khata-property-tax-documents).

## Documents to ask the seller for
The checklist depends on the property type and the transaction. It may include title deeds and the Mother Deed, the Encumbrance Certificate, revenue records (RTC / Pahani, mutation), approvals (RERA, BBMP / BDA / BMRDA, OC / CC), Khata and tax records, survey documents and development-rights documents. A specialist sends a checklist for the specific property.
