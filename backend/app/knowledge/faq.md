# Frequently asked questions

## Can you tell me whether a property is safe to buy?
The advocate reviews the available evidence and explains material risks, missing information and next steps, with a Go / Investigate / Do Not Proceed verdict. No service can guarantee that every possible risk or dispute will be found.

## Are stamp duty and registration fees included?
No. Stamp duty, registration fees and other government charges are billed separately at actuals. Package prices cover Homexo Legal's own work only.

## Do status updates mean the government portal is live-connected?
No. Status updates reflect the case team's recorded progress and confirmed source checks. There is no live connection to government portals.

## Where does Homexo Legal work?
Homexo Legal focuses on property in Bengaluru and Karnataka, including BBMP, BDA and BMRDA areas.

## How do I pay?
Fees are confirmed by the specialist on the callback before any work starts. The chat assistant cannot take payments.

## How do I reach a person?
Request a free callback on the website or in the chat and a specialist will call. Customers with an open request can also message their case team from Track your request or from the chat after verifying their ticket.
