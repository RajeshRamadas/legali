# Buyer packages

## Package overview and prices
Homexo Legal offers three fixed-fee packages for home buyers in Bengaluru and Karnataka.
- Shield Starter: ₹4,999, delivered in 48–72 hours.
- Shield Pro: ₹9,999, delivered in 5–7 working days. This is the most popular package.
- Shield Complete: ₹18,999, delivered in 7–10 days, plus post-purchase support.
Stamp duty, registration fees and other government charges are not included in any package. They are billed separately at actuals.
A specialist can discuss the property type, the transaction stage and the support needed before the customer picks a package.

## Shield Starter (₹4,999)
Shield Starter is a quick first check, delivered in 48–72 hours. It includes:
- Title deed verification
- Encumbrance Certificate (EC) check
- RERA registration check
- Basic litigation check
- A one-page Go / No-Go verdict PDF
- WhatsApp status updates
- One video consultation session
Starter does not include SRO field verification, sale agreement drafting or Khata transfer support.

## Shield Pro (₹9,999)
Shield Pro is the most popular package, delivered in 5–7 working days. It includes everything in Starter plus:
- A 30-year title history check
- Mother Deed chain verification
- BBMP / BDA / BMRDA approval audit
- Land acquisition notice check
- SRO physical field verification
- A buyer-protective sale agreement
- Khata and property tax records
- Two video consultation sessions
Pro does not include executing the Khata transfer. That is part of Shield Complete.

## Shield Complete (₹18,999)
Shield Complete covers the purchase end to end, delivered in 7–10 days plus post-purchase support. It includes everything in Pro plus:
- Sale deed drafting and registration support
- Khata transfer (BBMP / BDA)
- Property tax name change
- BESCOM name change support
- Aadhaar eSign support for documents
- A secure document vault
- A 12-month post-purchase helpline
- A dedicated advocate assigned to the case
- Three video consultation sessions

## Choosing a package
Starter suits a buyer who wants a fast first opinion before negotiating. Pro suits most resale and new-apartment purchases because it adds the 30-year title history, Mother Deed chain, approvals audit and an SRO field check. Complete suits buyers who also want the sale deed, registration and the post-purchase transfers (Khata, property tax, BESCOM) handled for them. When unsure, the customer can request a free callback and a specialist will recommend one.

## Advocate consultation (₹999)
A 30-minute video consultation with a senior property advocate costs ₹999. It includes a written summary of the advice, and the fee is credited toward a Shield package ordered within 7 days.
